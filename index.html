RC CAR

  1. On-road RC cars:Designed for smooth surfaces and high speeds.
  2. Off-road buggies:Great for dirt, gravel, and rough terrain.
  3. Monster trucks:Large tires, high ground clearance, and excellent for jumps.
  4. Rock crawlers:Built for climbing rocks and steep obstacles.
  5. Drift cars:Specially designed for controlled sideways drifting on smooth surfaces.

Power types

  • Electric Brushed motor:Affordable, easy to maintain, ideal for beginners.
  • Electric Brushless motor:Faster, more efficient, and more durable.
  • Nitro:Uses fuel, realistic engine sound, but requires more maintenance.
  • Gas-powered: Usually larger scale (1/5), powerful, and expensive.

Basic components

  1. Transmitter (remote controller)
  2. Receiver
  3. ESC Electronic Speed Controller
  4. Steering servo
  5. Battery (commonly LiPo)
  6. Chassis
  7. Suspension
  8. Tires and wheels
